If you have not completed by 1 September 2025 a Finnish matriculation examination, an IB or EB examination, or an RP or DIA examination administered in Finland, or the required Open University studies, you must apply in admission group 1. More information can be found on the admission’s Studyinfo page Bachelor's Programme in Science, Admission group 1, Bachelor and Master of Science (3 yrs + 2 yrs).

You must also demonstrate that you are proficient in English in the required manner. For further instructions, see the section “Required attachments”.

You may accept only one study place beginning in the same academic term in a study programme leading to a higher education degree in Finland.
